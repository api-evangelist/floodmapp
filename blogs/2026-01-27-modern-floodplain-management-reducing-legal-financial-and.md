Floodplain management isn’t just a regulatory checkbox—it’s a high-stakes enterprise risk decision. When legal, finance, floodplain, and emergency management teams operate from different data and assumptions, organizations become exposed to legal scrutiny, funding delays, and fractured response. Explore why traditional, static flood maps fall short—and how impact-based flood intelligence creates a shared, defensible view of flood impacts that leaders can trust before, during, and after an event.
